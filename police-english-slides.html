別驚慌！發生什麼事？Don't Panic! What's Happened?

句型01-11：報案與初步應對

情境：Hotline Service（119報案電話服務）

#English中文
01Hello, this is 119 of the Greenlake County Fire Department. How can I help you?您好。這裡是翠湖縣政府消防局。如何協助您？
02Don't panic! What's happened?別驚慌！發生什麼事？
03Calm down. I am here to help you.請冷靜。讓我來協助您。
04Please tell me your name and the location of the emergency.請告訴我您的姓名及緊急事故所在位址。
05Are there any landmarks around you?附近有什麼顯著的地標嗎？
06We will dispatch a firefighting team immediately.好！我們馬上派人過去！
07Please evacuate the scene instantly.請即刻遠離火災現場。
08Leave your passports and things where they are. Don't go back to get them.請不要再返回火場去拿取您們的護照及財物。
09The fire trucks and firefighters will be arriving any moment now.消防車及消防人員很快便會抵達現場。
10Let me perform an initial assessment of your injuries. Please relax and stay calm.我現在會先幫您的身體做一次初步創傷評估。請放輕鬆並保持鎮定。
11We'll place you on a stretcher and put you in the ambulance. After that we will check your breathing, pulse and blood pressure.我們現在要將您移至擔架上，並搬運至救護車內。待會我們將量測您的呼吸、脈搏及血壓。
記憶順序：接電話問候（01）→ 安撫別慌（02-03）→ 問姓名地點（04-05）→ 派遣＋撤離（06-08）→ 抵達＋救護（09-11）

情境模擬：Practice and Play（119報案 → 到場救護）

John 在民宿發現火災報案，消防隊到場後 EMT 為受傷的 Catherine 進行初步救護。

You：Hello, this is the 119 of the Greenlake County Fire Department. How can I help you?

John：$#%%&*&#……（慌亂中無法清楚表達）

You：Don't panic! What's happened?

John：Fire! Fire! There is a fire!

You：Calm down. I am here to help you. Where are you and what is on fire? Please tell me your name and the location of the emergency.

John：Cozy Green Guesthouse. （地標：近圓環公車總站）

You：Do you know the name of the bed and breakfast? Are there any landmarks around you? OK. We will dispatch a firefighting team immediately. Please evacuate the scene instantly. Leave them where they are, don't go back to get them. The fire trucks and firefighters will be arriving soon.

John：We didn't have time to take our passports and things!

防災無國界 Disaster Preparedness Without Borders

句型01-13：地震避難三步驟＋避難包

情境：Public Awareness & Public Education for Disaster Risk Reduction（新住民／移工防災宣導）

#English中文
01Don't panic! Being in Taiwan, you have to get used to earthquakes and typhoons.別驚慌！在臺灣生活要能夠適應，時常出現颱風及地震的環境。
02I would like to lead everyone in a disaster preparedness drill on taking refuge during an earthquake.我希望今天能帶領大家進行地震避難的防災演練。
03Keep in mind these three words when you feel a quake: Drop, Cover, and Hold.請記得地震發生時避難的三步驟：趴下、掩護及穩住。
04If there are not any sturdy tables nearby, then you can take refuge under some other strong furniture, or solid structural beams, with your hands over your neck and head for protection.如果身邊沒有堅固的桌子，可以躲在相對堅固的傢俱、樑柱或牆壁角落，以手保護頸部跟頭部。
05Since we tend to get injured by falling objects during earthquakes, the first step is to squat down, then find a protective shield, and hold on to something to steady yourself.高處掉落的物體可能使我們受傷，第一步「蹲下」，接下來找「掩護」，最後握住堅固物體「穩住」自己！
06The correct action is not to crouch down but to kneel down and hold on the table's legs instead of covering your head with your hands.正確動作是採雙膝跪姿並緊握桌腳，而不是蹲下用手護頭。
07When assisting people in wheelchairs during earthquakes, you need to first lock the wheels, then protect the person's head or neck, and maintain a sitting posture until the quaking stops.輪椅行動不便者：先鎖車輪，再護頭頸，並保持坐姿直至震動停止。
08Learning proper methods for taking refuge is the best way to protect yourself and your family.學會正確的避難方法，是保護自己及守護家人之最佳方式。
09Food and water are the most basic supplies, and you will need a minimum of three days' worth.緊急糧食以乾糧跟水為必要，準備約3日份。
10Other necessary items in the emergency kit are thermal blankets, raincoats, and towels, for keeping your body warm.保溫毛毯、雨衣及毛巾，可在避難時禦寒保暖。
11Medical and hygiene supplies are also necessary items to be included in the emergency kit — disinfectant, sanitizer, face masks, toilet paper and personal medications.醫療及清潔消毒用品：殺菌劑、除菌劑、口罩、衛生紙及個人藥物。
12I would also like to remind you to check and replace all perishable or expired items in the emergency kit every six months.緊急避難包裡的物品，每半年要定期檢查並更新淘汰過期物品。
13Keep the kit in a handy spot for easy fetching. With the kit in hand, plus a helmet and shoes, you are all set.將避難包放置在便利拿取的地方，另放置安全帽及鞋子，即完成最好的準備。
地震避難口訣：Drop, Cover, Hold（趴下、掩護、穩住）；桌邊正確動作是跪不是蹲；輪椅者先鎖輪再護頭頸。

結語：為民服務必備八句話

Do You Think in English or Chinese? — 服務台常用8句通用句型（At the Service Counter），不限警察／消防／海巡，各機關為民服務櫃檯皆可套用。

(1) 問需求：Anything I can do for you? / How may I help you? / What can I do for you?　請問今天要辦理什麼業務？
(2) 請取號：Please take a number and wait for the number to be called.　請先抽號碼牌等待叫號。
(3) 致歉久候：Thank you for your patience.　不好意思，讓您久等了。
(4) 指引表單：Please request the registration form from the service desk.　您可以至服務臺索取登記表。
(5) 說明文件要求：I need three one-inch color ID photos taken within the past two years.　我需要本人最近2年內拍攝之1吋脫帽五官清晰正面半身彩色照片3張。
(6) 確認還有無其他需求：Is there anything else I can do for you? Anything else?　請問還有其他需要服務的地方嗎？
(7) 說明時程：The application process will take up three working days.　證件申辦需要3個工作天。
(8) 留下聯繫方式：Please feel free to contact me if you have any further questions. I can be reached by this direct line. I am at your service.　有任何問題可以打這個專線給我，我很樂意為您服務。
Q：這8句話跟前兩單元的差別？
A：前兩單元（緊急救護／防災宣導）是特定情境（119報案、地震避難）的專業對話；這8句話則是任何為民服務櫃檯都通用的萬用句型，考試時可作為「服務台情境」題型的共同基礎。
